Beyond the desk

Some of what I bring is not on a job description. A theater degree and improv training are why I am comfortable in a room and quick on my feet when a filing goes sideways at 4:45. Graphic design and website work show up whenever a form, a reference sheet, or an internal guide could be clearer than it is. And a long habit of building small tools — workflow guides, court-link references, tracking databases — comes from finding the friction in a process genuinely interesting rather than annoying.
