How I work

A pleading is making an argument. If I am following that argument as it builds, I catch things a straight proofread would not.

I would rather understand a document than process it. That is where the real errors surface — a position in one section that quietly conflicts with something argued three pages later, or a missing word that inverts the meaning of a sentence.
